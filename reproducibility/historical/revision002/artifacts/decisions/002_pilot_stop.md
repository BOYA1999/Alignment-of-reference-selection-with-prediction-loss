# Decision 002 — close `pilot_001` as a verified negative pilot

Date: 2026-09-11  
Decision: `STOP_PILOT_NEGATIVE`  
Next allowed state: `GO_FORENSIC_DIAGNOSTIC_AND_NEW_TOPIC_AUDIT`

## Decision

The pilot is scientifically interpretable and technically valid, but its prespecified AIPW recovery claim failed Gate 6. The current confirmation campaign is closed. This decision is not revised by the passing coverage interval, because the point-estimate recovery threshold was a conjunctive requirement and the intervals were broad.

## What remains valid

- Selective reference availability was strongly predictable from frozen molecular covariates for all five masks.
- Complete-case evaluation showed practically relevant risk distortion in multiple actual and simulated MAR settings.
- The released early/late split, structures, outcomes, features, and result grids passed integrity checks.
- The negative AIPW finding is broad across seeds, endpoints, anchors, and models and is not an isolated numerical explosion.

## What is stopped

- The claim that the frozen AIPW procedure improves full-cohort MAE recovery under MAR.
- The conditional 200-seed confirmation expansion under the same procedure.
- Application of that procedure to real sparse endpoints as an accepted correction.
- PXR external work and D-MPNN expansion under the old `GO_FULL` route.
- Any threshold relaxation, favorable row deletion, or estimator/model addition intended to reverse the pilot decision.

## Permitted diagnostic, not a rescue analysis

Before selecting a replacement Original Article route, a new frozen diagnostic may:

1. replay row-level formulas for prespecified MCAR and MAR scenarios;
2. compare estimated with known generator propensities to localize propensity versus augmentation failure;
3. compare the full observation representation with one prespecified lower-dimensional covariate block without changing the pilot decision.

Its output may support or reject a new hypothesis about high-dimensional nuisance instability. It cannot retroactively convert `pilot_001` to `GO_FULL`.

## Publication gate

The verified negative pilot alone is insufficient for a JPA Original Article. A replacement route must pass a fresh primary-literature collision audit, establish a domain-specific contribution beyond known finite-sample AIPW caveats, freeze untouched confirmation evidence, and retain the pilot as development evidence.
